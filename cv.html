Research experience

Master’s research, Peking University2022 – 2026
Advisor Prof. Bin Chen; in collaboration with Ye Shen
  • Extended the Comisso–Asenjo process to plasma plunging inside the ISCO and to arbitrary plasmoid ejection angles; introduced the covering factor as a measure of an accretion flow’s extraction capability.
  • Treated reconnection-model parameters as free and defined a reconnection-rate-weighted covering factor, showing that the Sweet–Parker limit overestimates energy extraction.
  • Showed that in a rotating Einstein–bumblebee spacetime, every stationary equatorial quantity depends on the Lorentz-violating parameter only through an effective spin.
  • Classified ejected plasmoids along geodesics as escaping, bound, or captured, which yields a spin floor of a ≈ 0.80, below which no energy-extracting plasmoid escapes at any magnetization.
